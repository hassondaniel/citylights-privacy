City Lights — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

City Lights is a calm lantern puzzle game published by Daniel Hasson (Doris). We collect as little as possible, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, and no analytics or tracking across apps or websites.
  • It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • You don't need an account or a sign-in to play.

What stays on your device

  • Your level progress, stars, oil, boosters, daily missions and settings are saved on the device.
  • Deleting the app deletes this local data.

Online features (friends, gifts and festivals)

When the game's online features are available, it uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) as its server. To make them work, the following is stored there:

  • An anonymous player ID created by the game (not your name, email or phone number).
  • The display name you choose in the game, if you choose one.
  • Your friends list, light you send to and receive from friends, and your lantern in your friends' night sky.
  • A backup of your progress, and the number of lanterns you light for your weekly festival group.
  • Reports you make about another player's name.

This data is used only to run these features. It is not used to identify you or to show you ads.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

  • You can choose to save your progress to your Apple ID, so it comes back on a new phone.
  • The game does not ask Apple for your name or email. Apple gives the game only an anonymous identifier, which is linked to your player ID.

Purchases

  • The game is free to play. Optional purchases (stars, the season pass and the Quiet Skies subscription) are sold through Apple's App Store.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your online account and everything stored with it. You can also write to us and we will delete it for you.

Children

City Lights is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
