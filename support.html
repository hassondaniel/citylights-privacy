City Lights — Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to hassondaniel2@gmail.com and we'll answer as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • How do I play? Drag across three or more touching lanterns of the same colour, in any direction, including diagonally. Long chains make special lanterns.
  • What is oil? Starting a level is free; only a failed level uses one oil. Oil refills by itself, one every 10 minutes.
  • I'm stuck on a level. Try a booster from the level screen. After a few tries the city gives you a little lantern blessing.
  • I bought something on another device. Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases.
  • Cancel the Quiet Skies subscription. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → City Lights.
  • Keep my progress on a new phone. Settings → Save your progress → Sign in with Apple (when online features are available).
  • Turn off the music. Settings → Music.

Your data

No ads and no tracking. You can delete your account in Settings → Delete my account. See the privacy policy.
